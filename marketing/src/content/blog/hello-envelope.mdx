Envelope started with a simple question — *where did the money go?*

Traditional budgeting apps tell you the answer **after** you've spent. We wanted the opposite. Decide where each dollar goes *before* it leaves your hands. That's zero-based budgeting, and it's what Envelope is built around.

## What's inside

- **Envelopes** for every spending category
- **Goals** that project debt payoff with real APR math
- **Shared budgets** for partners and households
- **Sync** across iOS, Android, and web

## What's next

We're working on AI-assisted categorization, deeper reports, and an open data export. Subscribe to the changelog to follow along.
